PhentiBuild Reference Card

The Camera Scanner measures timber and walls by comparing them with an object of known size in the same photo. Print this card, or simply use any bank or ID card: they are all exactly the same size (85.6 mm × 54 mm). For walls, an A4 sheet of paper works better.

How to print it correctly
  1. Click Print below. In the print settings choose Actual size / 100% scale, not "Fit to page".
  2. Check with a ruler: the black-and-white bar on the card must measure exactly 50 mm. If it doesn't, the card will give wrong measurements.
  3. Cut one card out and glue it to stiff cardboard so it stays flat.
  4. On site, hold the card flat against the timber or wall, fit everything in the frame, and photograph straight on in good light.
